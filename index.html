Questions from
the deep.

Is Sinker free?

Yes. The app is free to download and the focus timer, fishing, tank, shop and journal are free forever. Sinker Pro is an optional one-time purchase.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. It's tied to your Apple ID, so you can restore it on a new iPhone, and Family Sharing can be turned on.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your iPhone and is backed up to your own iCloud so you can restore it later.

Does it track me?

No tracking, no ads, no analytics. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a rough location for the optional Real weather feature. Read the privacy policy.

What happens if I leave early?

You can reel in any time. Your line only got as deep as you stayed, so you'll catch something from that depth, or trash if it was under three minutes. You still get your break.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Sinker is iPhone first. This web timer works on any device in the meantime.
