Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Sinker is a focus timer and fishing game made by Fourpixels. It's built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains the little data the app uses and where it goes. Effective November 24, 2026.

The short version

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your progress stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud backup. We don't run servers that receive it.

The only thing that ever reaches anyone other than you and Apple is an approximate location for the optional Real weather feature, and only if you turn it on.

What stays on your device

Game progress (coins, catches, tank, journal), settings, focus history and streaks are stored only on your iPhone. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a copy of this through an app group on the same device.

Motion data for the tilt effect is read live from your phone's sensors to move the picture. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Alarms and reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

iCloud backup

If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is saved to your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so you can restore it on a new iPhone or after reinstalling. Apple stores it under your Apple ID; we never receive or see it.

You can back up now, restore, or turn backups off in Journal → About → iCloud backup. Turning off iCloud for Sinker in iOS Settings stops it too.

Real weather (optional)

If you turn on Real weather, the app asks iOS for your location at the lowest accuracy, rounds it to about 10 km (0.1°), and sends only those rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current weather. Nothing else is sent with it, and we never see or keep your location.

You can turn it off at any time in the app (tackle sheet → Real weather) or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Sinker. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to their service.

Purchases

Sinker Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. The app only asks Apple's StoreKit, on your device, whether this Apple ID owns Sinker Pro.

Children

Sinker isn't directed at children under 13, and since it doesn't collect personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect any from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

Everything is on your device and in your own iCloud, so you're in control: use Journal → About → Reset all progress to wipe this iPhone, turn iCloud backup off there too, or delete the app to remove its data from the phone. The iCloud copy can be deleted in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Location and notification access can be changed in iOS Settings at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
